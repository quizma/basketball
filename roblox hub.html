Roblox


Roblox is an online gaming platform and game creation system. Unlike traditional video games, it does not come with a single built-in game. Instead, it functions as a massive, user-generated virtual universe where players can explore millions of different 3D experiences created by other users.



Wanna Play Roblox?


RetroStudio is a popular Roblox game that allows players to create, share, and play classic-style Roblox experiences from the 2009 to 2015 era. It features a built-in 2013-style studio, visual scripting, and a nostalgic avatar editor.
